WHY FOODBRIDGE

When food is shared,
everybody eats better.

Restaurants, hotels, event hosts, households and local growers often have good food left over. FoodBridge gives it a safe, simple route from surplus to community tables, shelters and neighborhood organizations.

Get to know the project
A simple chain of care

From extra to essential.

Clear handoffs help every donation arrive safely, on time, and ready to share.

01

Donor reports surplus

Food, quantity, expiry and pickup details are added to a listing.

02

Donation goes live

The new listing is marked available for volunteers.

03

Volunteer accepts

One volunteer takes responsibility for the collection.

04

Food is collected

The volunteer confirms the pickup from the donor.

05

Food is distributed

Meals reach a shelter, NGO or community group.

06

Donation is completed

The final handoff and people served are recorded.
