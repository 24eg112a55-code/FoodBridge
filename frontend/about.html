OUR MISSION

Good food should
find good people.

Community Food Sharing System is a simple coordination platform for turning safe surplus food into local support. Donors post what they can share; volunteers organize collection and distribution to community groups, shelters and neighbors.

Better together, one pickup at a time.

The project brings donors, volunteers and administrators into one accountable workflow. Clear pickup details, status updates and distribution records help food move with care and make each contribution visible.
